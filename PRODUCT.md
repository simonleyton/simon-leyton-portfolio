# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design directors, and recruiters evaluating a senior product designer. They skim first (10–30 seconds on the hero and case-study covers), then deep-read one or two case studies before an interview. Secondary: peers and collaborators checking credibility.

## Product Purpose

Simon Leyton's portfolio. The site is itself a work sample: it must prove senior product-design craft (strategy, delivery, experimentation, IA) through both its case-study content and its own execution. Success = a reader leaves believing Simon sets direction AND ships, and can defend every claim in an interview.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, precise. The Miami-sky pixel-art hero carries personality; everything layered on it stays quiet and disciplined (glass cards, warm paper, restrained type). Editorial voice: conversational but exact, outcome-shaped, no resume-speak.

## Anti-references

- The gabrielvaldivia.com template it started from: must not read as a recognizable clone.
- AI-template scaffolding: uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, coral accent overuse, generic SaaS landing patterns.
- Clip-art / metaphor illustration in work slots: every work image shows real product UI (canonical iPhone screen-transplant spec).
- Overclaiming: no invented metrics, projections labeled as projections, criteria never framed as results.

## Design Principles

1. The work is the hero: no throat-clearing masthead, work on screen within the first viewport.
2. One system, executed identically: card images, kickers, captions, and stat rows follow single specs site-wide.
3. Integrity is a design feature: every number on the site survives interview questioning.
4. Quiet chrome, loud content: the signature background and product imagery carry the personality; the framing layer stays neutral.
5. Evidence over metaphor: show the actual screen, the actual sketch, the actual research quote.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body/muted text (muted tokens tuned 0.66 light / 0.82 dark). Keyboard support on interactive surfaces (carousel arrow keys, focus-visible coral rings). Descriptive alt text on all case-study imagery. Dark mode fully supported, including imagery (transparent card canvases).
